# Instagram Content Strategy for Tap 2 Invite

Here is a detailed content strategy to help you grow your Instagram following and drive traffic to your website.

## 1. Content Pillars

Your content should revolve around these four pillars:

### Pillar 1: Template Showcase (60% of your content)

This is the core of your content. You want to showcase your beautiful invitation templates in the best possible light.

*   **High-quality mockups:** Create realistic mockups of your invitations on different devices (phones, tablets, desktops).
*   **Animated previews:** Since your invitations are animated, create short video previews of the animations.
*   **User-generated content:** Encourage your customers to share photos and videos of their invitations and tag you. Repost this content (with permission) to your feed.

### Pillar 2: Event Inspiration (20% of your content)

Inspire your followers with ideas for their events. This will help you build a community around your brand.

*   **Color palettes:** Create and share color palettes that match your invitation templates.
*   **Decoration ideas:** Share photos and videos of event decorations that complement your templates.
*   **Event planning tips:** Share tips and tricks for planning a successful event.

### Pillar 3: Behind the Scenes (10% of your content)

Show your followers the human side of your business. This will help you build trust and a stronger connection with your audience.

*   **The design process:** Share a behind-the-scenes look at how you create a new template.
*   **Your workspace:** Share a photo of your workspace to give your followers a glimpse into your world.
*   **Meet the team:** If you have a team, introduce them to your followers.

### Pillar 4: Educational Content (10% of your content)

Help your followers get the most out of your product.

*   **Editor tutorials:** Create short video tutorials that show how to use your invitation editor.
*   **"How-to" guides:** Create guides on topics like "How to choose the perfect invitation for your wedding" or "How to word your baby shower invitation."

## 2. Content Formats

Use a mix of content formats to keep your feed interesting and engaging.

*   **Single Image Posts:** For showcasing a single template or a beautiful event photo.
*   **Carousel Posts:** For showing multiple angles of a template, or for creating "before and after" posts. You can also use carousels for mini-tutorials.
*   **Reels:** For short, engaging videos that can go viral. This is the perfect format for showing off your animated invitations.
*   **Stories:** For more informal, behind-the-scenes content, polls, Q&As, and for driving traffic to your website with the "link" sticker.

## 3. Posting Cadence

Consistency is key on Instagram. I recommend starting with **3-4 posts per week**.

*   **Monday:** Template Showcase (Reel)
*   **Wednesday:** Event Inspiration (Carousel Post)
*   **Friday:** Template Showcase (Single Image Post)
*   **Weekend:** Behind the Scenes or Educational Content (Story)

## 4. Hashtag Strategy

Use a mix of broad, niche, and branded hashtags to reach a wider audience.

*   **Broad hashtags (1-5):** #digitalinvitations #animatedinvitations #eventinvitations #weddinginvitations #partyinvitations
*   **Niche hashtags (5-10):** #quinceanera-invitations #babyshowerinvitations #modernweddinginvitations #birthdayinvitation-ideas #eventplanning
*   **Branded hashtags (1-2):** #tap2invite #tap2invite-creations

## 5. Call to Action (CTA)

Every post should have a clear call to action.

*   "Like this post if you love this template!"
*   "Comment below with your favorite color palette."
*   "Share this with a friend who's planning a party."
*   "Click the link in our bio to create your own invitation."

By following this content strategy, you'll be well on your way to growing your Instagram following and driving more traffic to your website. Good luck!
