# Next Steps: Digital Invitations Platform

This document outlines potential business enhancements and strategic next steps for the digital invitations platform, based on a business analyst review.

---

### 1. User Engagement & Growth

*   **Social Sharing & Virality:**
    *   **Action:** Implement one-click sharing of invitations to WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, etc.
    *   **Action:** Add Open Graph meta tags (`og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`) to invitation pages for rich link previews.

*   **Guest Communication Tools:**
    *   **Action:** Develop a feature for hosts to send updates/reminders to their guest list (Potential Premium Feature).
    *   **Action:** Allow guests to contact the host through the platform.

*   **Referral Program:**
    *   **Action:** Create a referral system offering discounts or credits for referring new customers.

---

### 2. Monetization & Service Expansion

*   **Tiered Pricing & Freemium Model:**
    *   **Action:** Solidify `Free`, `Premium`, and `Business` tiers with distinct feature sets.
    *   **Action:** Expand the `CorporateTemplate` into a full "Business" offering with features like branding control and ticketing integration.

*   **Post-Event Services:**
    *   **Action:** Introduce a collaborative photo gallery for guests to use after the event.
    *   **Action:** Offer a feature to design and send digital "Thank You" cards.

---

### 3. Product & Feature Enhancement

*   **AI-Powered Assistance:**
    *   **Action:** Integrate a language model for AI-assisted content generation (e.g., invitation wording).
    *   **Action:** Use AI to suggest themes, color palettes, or generate unique header images.

*   **Collaborative Editing:**
    *   **Action:** Allow multiple users to co-edit a single invitation.

*   **Analytics Dashboard for Hosts:**
    *   **Action:** Provide hosts with analytics on RSVP trends and guest engagement.

---

### Summary of Recommendations

The platform has a strong foundation. The recommended next steps are to:
1.  **Prioritize quick wins:** Enhancing social sharing can provide immediate growth.
2.  **Optimize monetization:** Formalize pricing tiers to increase revenue.
3.  **Invest in strategic features:** AI and collaborative editing will create a long-term competitive advantage.
